Waterfall enrichment (recommended approach)

Try free/cheap sources first, fall back to paid only when needed. Most contacts resolved at $0.

1. Apollo Free — user's existing Apollo DB ~60-70% hit rate
2. PDL $0.10/record — large coverage ~20% more
3. LeadMagic $0.50/company — role search + verify remainder
Cost optimization: If Apollo covers 70% of contacts for free, and PDL gets another 20% at $0.10 each — the average cost per enriched contact drops to ~$0.02. Only the hardest-to-find contacts hit the expensive providers.
