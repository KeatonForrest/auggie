About Auggie

What is Auggie?

Auggie is an AI-powered account research platform built for B2B sales teams. It automates the deep company research that reps need before outreach — pulling from public sources like company websites, job postings, SEC filings, and technology signals to produce structured, actionable intelligence.

How it works

  • You provide a company URL.
  • Auggie scrapes publicly available information: homepage, careers page, blog, news, regulatory filings, and technology stack.
  • AI models analyze the data and produce a structured research document: company overview, business problems, hiring signals, tech stack, opportunity scoring, and personalized talking points.
  • Results are delivered via the web app, API, or integrated directly into your CRM.

Data practices

  • Public data only: Auggie researches companies using publicly available web content. We do not scrape private or gated content.
  • No personal data harvesting: We do not collect or sell personal information about individuals. Contact enrichment is opt-in and uses licensed third-party providers.
  • Secure infrastructure: All traffic is encrypted via TLS. OAuth tokens and credentials are stored encrypted at rest. We do not store credit card numbers (handled by Stripe).
