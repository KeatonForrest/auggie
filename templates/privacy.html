Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Auggie ("the Service"), we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name and email address provided via Google or Microsoft OAuth sign-in.
  • Usage data: Company URLs you research, lists you create, and research results generated.
  • Integration data: OAuth tokens for third-party services you connect (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets). We store tokens securely and only use them to perform actions you request.
  • Payment information: Billing is processed by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Service
  • Process your research requests and deliver results
  • Import data from connected integrations at your request
  • Process payments and manage your account
  • Send transactional communications (e.g., receipts, alerts you configure)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, payment processing (Stripe), and AI model providers (Anthropic) that help us operate the Service.
  • Third-party integrations: Only when you explicitly connect and use them (e.g., pushing data to your CRM).

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), secure token storage, and access controls. OAuth tokens for integrations are stored encrypted at rest.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and research results for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Disconnect any third-party integrations at any time
  • Delete your account by contacting us

7. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at keaton@auggie.tools.
